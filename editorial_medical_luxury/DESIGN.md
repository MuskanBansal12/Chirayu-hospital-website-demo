## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial medical luxury identity tailored for specialized gastrointestinal and hepatology clinical care. Bridging clinical rigor with architectural serenity, the aesthetic elevates regional healthcare into an authoritative, tranquil, and world-class consultation experience.

### Visual Pillars
- **Serene Authority:** Deep forest undertones and surgical teals replace clinical, sterile blues, imparting profound grounding, clinical depth, and trust.
- **Architectural Framing:** Crisp lines, ample negative space, and disciplined grids evoke modern wellness pavilions and institutional permanence.
- **Understated Opulence:** Tactile warm ivory backdrops paired with muted champagne accents provide warmth, mitigating medical anxiety while conferring bespoke medical attention.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical precision with quiet hospitality. 

- **Primary (`#102D28` - Deep Midnight Green):** Grounding foundation used for authoritative display headers, primary structural containers, and dominant CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#176B64` - Medical Teal):** Active clinical accents, interactive hover states, verified statuses, and medical subheadings.
- **Tertiary (`#C5AA7A` - Muted Champagne):** Reserved for accreditation seals, doctor credentials, badge outlines, and micro-accents.
- **Neutral (`#F7F5EF` - Warm Ivory):** The canvas base, softening high-contrast elements and eradicating sterile optical glare.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `soft-sage` (`#DDE7DF`): Secondary container fills, table alternating row highlights, and calm indicator backgrounds.
  - `charcoal` (`#202724`): High-legibility body text and data values.
  - `ivory-surface` (`#FDFCF9`): Elevated card and modal backgrounds.
  - `champagne-light` (`#F3EDE2`): Tonal badge containers and subtle active states.

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of Playfair Display with the analytical, geometric clarity of Manrope and Inter.

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Handled exclusively with Playfair Display. Headlines should lean toward natural editorial casing with snug letter spacing to maintain prestige.
- **Narratives & Patient Information:** Body copy uses Manrope for fluid reading experiences and humanistic proportions.
- **Clinical Data, Timings & Status Badges:** Set in Inter with uppercase treatment, slight tracking, and mid-weights to ensure immediate scanning across medical diagnostics, operating hours, and consultation rosters.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column architectural grid forms the baseline on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Architectural Rhythms:** Vertical cadence relies on open space to communicate calm and order. Section modules utilize generous gutters and breathing room to prevent clinical density.
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Canvas outer borders enforce wide margins (`3rem` on desktop), establishing an editorial border framing effect.
- **Content Blocks:** Hero, department highlights, and physician credentials span asymmetric divisions (e.g., 5-column profile alongside 7-column procedural data) to reinforce an editorial narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects high-intensity drop shadows in favor of architectural layering and subtle glassmorphic translucency.

- **Glassmorphic Shields:** Fixed navigation bars, floating booking cards, and critical appointment summaries use an ivory glass technique (`rgba(253, 252, 249, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a 1px border of `rgba(23, 107, 100, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Elevation:** Level changes occur through tonal shifts between Warm Ivory canvas (`#F7F5EF`), elevated card surfaces (`#FDFCF9`), and soft sage recesses (`#DDE7DF`).
- **Diffuse Ambient Depth:** Modals and critical floating alerts cast a soft, diffused ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(16, 45, 40, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, restrained curvature (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline) conveying structural stability, medical precision, and architectural permanence.

- **Cards & Diagnostic Containers:** Crisp corners softened with `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii.
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Select micro-elements (e.g., department badges, accreditation pills) utilize full pill contours (`9999px`) to juxtapose cleanly against the architectural geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Midnight Green (`#102D28`) with Warm Ivory typography, subtle 1px border (`rgba(197, 170, 122, 0.3)`), and `rounded` corners. Subtle lift on hover with secondary teal fill shift.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base framed with 1.5px Medical Teal (`#176B64`) outline, teal typography, transitioning to Soft Sage fill on hover.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Champagne or Teal text paired with a delicate inline arrow, underlined by an animated 1px hairline rule.

### Badges & Credentials
- Enclosed tags combining Warm Ivory surfaces, thin Champagne border outlines (`#C5AA7A`), and uppercase Inter label typography. Designed for qualifications such as "MD, DM (Gastroenterology)" and department markers.

### Cards & Information Panels
- Ivory-surface base (`#FDFCF9`) bounded by an ultra-thin 1px border (`#DDE7DF`).
- Header areas feature a hairline divider separating doctor profile abstracts from consultation hours and diagnostic equipment specs.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background filled with Soft Sage at 30% opacity or pure Ivory.
- 1px border in Medical Teal at 20% resting opacity, focusing to full `#176B64` with a gentle 2px champagne ring accent.
- Labels pinned in crisp uppercase Inter (`label-sm`).

### Lists & Timetables
- OPD Schedules and diagnostic tests structured as minimalist tables separated by subtle hairline rules (`#DDE7DF`), alternating with light sage hover rows.
