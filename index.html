A Local Address for Specialised Digestive Care.

Prior to the establishment of Chirayu Swasthya Gastro & Liver Hospital, patients across Hanumangarh, Sri Ganganagar, and neighboring districts often had to travel hundreds of kilometers to Jaipur or Delhi for emergency GI bleeding interventions, liver cirrhosis monitoring, and advanced diagnostic endoscopy.

Founded with architectural clarity and academic rigor by Dr. Hitesh Sharma, our facility integrates high-resolution video endoscopy systems, a fully automated pathology lab, an acute inpatient recovery ward, and an unhurried, evidence-based consultation desk.
